# Business Recommendations

## 1. Stop Treating All Discounts as Equal

Discounted customers generated lower average order value than non-discounted customers in the cleaned dataset. Discounts should not be used as a blanket revenue lever. Shift to targeted discounting for customers in the "At Risk" and "Low Engagement" segments, where an incentive can support reactivation.

Recommended action:
Use discounts only when a customer has high churn risk or when the offer is tied to a higher-margin category bundle.

Expected impact:
Better margin control and fewer unnecessary discounts for customers who would likely purchase without one.

## 2. Convert High-Value Non-Subscribers

Subscribers show stronger estimated lifetime value, while many non-subscribers still have strong previous purchase behavior. These customers are ideal subscription conversion targets because they already show shopping intent.

Recommended action:
Create a campaign for non-subscribers with previous_purchases >= 38 and high estimated CLV. Offer loyalty points, free shipping, or early sale access instead of a broad price discount.

Expected impact:
Higher retention and recurring purchase behavior without training customers to wait for discounts.

## 3. Prioritize Clothing as the Revenue Engine, Then Cross-Sell Accessories

Clothing is the largest revenue category. Accessories has the second-highest revenue and a strong CLV profile, making it a practical cross-sell category.

Recommended action:
Bundle Clothing with Accessories in seasonal campaigns. For example, show recommended accessories after a Clothing purchase or create "complete the outfit" offers.

Expected impact:
Higher basket value and stronger category attachment.

## 4. Build Retention Plays Around Purchase Frequency

Customers buying Quarterly, Every 3 Months, or Annually have longer gaps between purchases. Non-subscribers in these groups represent a churn-risk pool.

Recommended action:
Trigger lifecycle campaigns before the expected repurchase window. For Quarterly customers, send a personalized reminder around day 70. For Annual customers, run a reactivation sequence well before the yearly cycle ends.

Expected impact:
Improved repeat purchase rate and earlier intervention before customers become inactive.

## 5. Use Segment-Specific Messaging

RFM segmentation shows different customer types. Champions should not receive the same message as Low Engagement customers.

Recommended action:
Use the following segment strategy:

- Champions: early access, premium bundles, exclusive product previews
- Loyal Growth: subscription conversion and cross-sell campaigns
- At Risk: personalized win-back offer with urgency
- Low Engagement: low-cost reactivation tests and preference discovery

Expected impact:
Higher campaign relevance and better marketing spend allocation.
